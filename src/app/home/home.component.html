Introduction

An infection or disease that can harm black pepper plants such as a fungal, oomycete, viral or insect induced condition, is known as a disease in black pepper leaves. For black pepper farmers and producers, these diseases can have a serious impact on the quality and yield of the black pepper. Common diseases and pests affecting black pepper leaves include Gall Thrips, Leaf Blight, Mosaic, Pollu Disease (Anthracnose), Powdery Mildew and Quick Wilt (Foot Rot / Phytophthora). These issues often lead to leaf curling, spotting, yellowing, defoliation, stunted growth and in severe cases, complete vine collapse, resulting in significant economic losses.
